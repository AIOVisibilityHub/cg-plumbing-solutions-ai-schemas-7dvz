When Professional Guidance Helps

Knowing when to call a professional for emergency leak repair can save you a lot of trouble and expense. Here are some key indicators:

  • Active, uncontrolled flow: If water is gushing or flowing rapidly and you can't shut it off, either at the fixture or the main water supply, this is a clear emergency. Uncontrolled water can quickly flood your home, causing extensive damage to flooring, walls, and foundations.
  • Concealed leaks (inside walls, ceilings, floors): If you suspect a leak hidden behind a wall, above a ceiling, or under the floor, don't delay. For gas water heaters, find the gas shut-off valve on the gas line leading to the heater, often near the bottom, and turn it to the 'OFF' position. You might also set the control dial on the unit itself to 'OFF' or 'PILOT'.
